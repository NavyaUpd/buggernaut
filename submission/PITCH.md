# BIJLI · submission page text

**Title:** BIJLI
**Tagline:** A monsoon night. The city's power is out. Someone has to climb the poles in the storm.
**Team:** Buggernaut (Hansika Grover, Navya Upadhyay, Shaurya Chandel), IIIT Hyderabad
**Jam:** TGC Game Jam @ Infinium '26 · Themes: Comic · Twist · Light
**Platform:** Browser (HTML5). Keyboard only, about 12–15 minutes.

## Pitch

**BIJLI** is a monsoon-night platformer about the people who bring the light back. Climb poles and splice snapped
cables as Crew 7, a lineworker in a city-wide blackout. Wherever you restore the light, the rainy night turns into a
child's crayon comic: clouds to stand on, wires to grind, and dangers turned into cartoons. Grab Chinni's drawings to
become BIJLI, the superhero she's drawing at home, or put the storm dragon to sleep. _Bijli_ means both electricity and
lightning.

## Why it matters

Every monsoon, the light comes back because lineworkers climb wet poles in the dark and repair the lines by hand,
skilled and dangerous work that almost nobody sees, and that people still call "not a job for women". BIJLI is inspired
by the linewomen of Telangana. You never fight anyone: the core action is a repair, the game teaches the real safety
rule (switch the line off at its breaker before you touch it), and every light you restore brings back the people it
serves: a chai stall, a medicine-shop fridge, a hospital ward. Play to the end. The last page of Chinni's comic says
who the hero really is.

## Themes in one line each

- **Light** is what changes the level: wherever power returns, new paths open.
- **Twist**: every repair is a twist of the cable, light twists dangers into comic twins, and the story has a twist.
- **Comic**: the lit world is a child's comic, and her drawings are the power-ups.

## Controls

A / D move · Space jump (hold to glide as BIJLI) · W / S climb · E interact (tap for breakers, hold to splice) ·
Esc or P pause (restart, skip room, mute, reduce flashing) · R restart room

## Accessibility

Reduce-flashing toggle (title screen F, and the pause menu), skip room from the start, 3 helmets per chapter with
splices kept, no timers.

## AI usage

Built with Claude Code (Anthropic) as the coding assistant: it wrote most of the code and drafted the room maps and
in-game text from the team's design, which the team directed, reviewed and playtested. All art is drawn in code and all
sound is synthesised at runtime; no image, music or sound generators and no third-party assets were used. Details in
`AI_DISCLOSURE.md` in the repository.

## Inspired by

The linewomen of Telangana, who climb poles in the storm so the rest of us have light. In 2022, Babburi Sirisha from
Siddipet became the first woman appointed junior lineman at the state's southern power distribution company.
